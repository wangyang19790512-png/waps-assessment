## Scoring Algorithm Reference

### Vessel Suitability (0–100)
| Factor | Max pts | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Vessel type | 25 | bulk_carrier=25, tanker=22, general_cargo=20, research=18, training=16, container=14, ferry=10, offshore=8, port_service=5, other=10 |
| Service speed | 20 | ≤10kn=20, ≤12=16, ≤15=10, ≤18=5, >18=2 |
| Vessel age | 15 | <8yr=15, <15=12, <20=8, <25=4, ≥25=2, unknown=8 |
| Deck area | 20 | ≥200m²=20, ≥100=15, ≥50=10, <50=5, unknown=10 |
| Operating days | 10 | ≥300=10, ≥250=8, ≥200=6, ≥150=4, <150=2, unknown=6 |
| Stability GM | 10 | ≥2m=10, ≥1.5=8, ≥1.0=5, <1.0=2, unknown=5 |
| Deductions | — | cargoConflict high=-10/med=-4; craneConflict high=-8/med=-3; bridgeVisibility high=-5/med=-2 |

### Route Wind (0–100)
| Factor | Max pts | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Avg true wind speed | 35 | ≥9m/s=35, ≥7=27, ≥5=17, ≥3=8, <3=2, unknown=15 |
| Favorable wind % | 35 | ≥65%=35, ≥50=27, ≥35=19, ≥20=11, <20=4, unknown=15 |
| Seasonal variability | 20 | low=20, medium=14, high=8, unknown=10 |
| Beam reach % | 10 | ≥40%=10, ≥25=8, ≥15=5, <15=2, unknown=5 |

effectiveWindUtilizationRatio = (favorableWindPercentage ?? 50) / 100 × 0.75

### Fuel Saving (default coefficients)
- Conservative: windUtil × 0.06 × 0.90 × 0.85
- Base: windUtil × 0.10 × 0.90 × 0.85
- Optimistic: windUtil × 0.18 × 0.90 × 0.85

### Economic Score
paybackBase < 3yr → 90; <5 → 75; <7 → 60; <10 → 45; <15 → 30; ≥15 or null → 15

### Shipping Operation (start 100, deduct)
cargoConflict: high=-25, med=-12, low=-4 | craneConflict: high=-20, med=-10, low=-3 | bridgeVisibility: high=-15, med=-7, low=-2 | portRestriction: high=-15, med=-7, low=-2 | scheduleSensitivity: high=-10, med=-5, low=-1 | insuranceConcern: high=-8, med=-3, low=-1 | crewSkill: high=+3, low=-5 | maintenanceCap: high=+3, low=-5

### Class Risk (start 100, deduct)
stabilityBooklet missing=-15 | structuralDrawings missing=-15 | GA missing=-10 | incliningTest missing=-5 | GM<1.0=-20, GM 1.0-1.5=-10 | additionalWeight<20t=-15, <50t=-5

### Stability Structure (sum, cap 100)
stabilityBooklet=20 | structuralDrawings=20 | incliningTest=15 | GM≥2=20, ≥1.5=15, ≥1.0=10, <1.0=3, unknown=10 | additionalWeight≥50=15, ≥20=10, <20=3 | deckLoad≥4=10, ≥2=7, <2=3

### Overall Score Weights
Vessel 20% + RouteWind 20% + Economic 20% + Operation 15% + ClassRisk 10% + Stability 10% + Policy 5%

### Rating
A=85-100, B=70-84, C=55-69, D=40-54, E<40
